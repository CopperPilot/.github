## Review a design

Hardware gets better with more eyes on it. Pick any board above and tell us what a senior engineer would flag.

- **Open an issue** for a questionable footprint, a tight clearance, a missing decoupling cap or a part you'd swap.
- **Send a pull request** with fixes, variants, or ports to other fabs and stackups.
- **Build one** and show us. Photos, bring-up notes and failures are all welcome.

Each repo ships its DRC/ERC reports and review renders, so you can check our work the way you'd check your own.
